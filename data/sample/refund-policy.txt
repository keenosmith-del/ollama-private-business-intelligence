Northstar Components — Refund and Returns Policy
Effective date: 1 January 2026

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery. Products must be unused and returned in their original packaging. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days after inspection. Defective items qualify for a prepaid return label. Custom-configured products are not refundable unless defective. Customer Support must record the order number and reason for return before issuing a return authorisation.
